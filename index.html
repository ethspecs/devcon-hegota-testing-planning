Lesson from Glamsterdam

One of the mistakes we made on our side on the EL was underestimating the complexity of 8037. The idea would be to include the more complex EIPs a lot earlier in the cycle.
Spencer, ACDT #97, 21 September 2026
  • Test the hardest interactions first: frames × FOCIL.
  • Then the complex single EIPs. Simple EIPs ride along later.

Source: ACDT #97 transcript, t=2092. The EELS tracker (#3664) states the same principle: "aiming to test the most complex EIPs first: frames x FOCIL interactions, then the more complex single EIPs."

One flow

From EIP to devnet

The release is the contract: clients, hive and kurtosis all pin to the same tag.Hive is shared: Specs writes the simulators, ethPandaOps runs the infra.

Branch model (Spencer, Mattermost 28 Sep): forks/bogota as the base, one eips/bogota/eip-xxxx branch per CFI EIP, devnets/<feature|fork>/<n> to combine, then devnets/hegota/0 merges the two lanes.

Ask the room: is the hand-off from release to kurtosis to devnet explicit enough? Who confirms that every client branch runs the release before launch?

bogota-devnet-1 and heze-devnet-1

What the first devnets taught us

Late spec changes

A consensus-breaking test release two days before launch. One EL client forked at the first keyed-nonce transaction.

RPC and tooling

Tracing and RPC endpoints failed on frame transactions in several clients. No spec tests cover them.

Config drift

Slot duration, blob schedule and IL deadline lived in five places that did not agree.

  • FOCIL has no censorship-resistance scenarios in assertoor or spamoor yet.
  • Fork-transition-only behaviour (8253) needs many devnets, not one.

Keep client names off the slide unless the bug is fixed and public. Details in Dan's ACDT #101 prep (vault): the fork was at block 1788 on bogota-devnet-1, intrinsic gas missing the 12,100 keyed-nonce access cost from tests-bogota-devnet@v1.1.0.

Public RPC issues: go-ethereum#35783 (callTracer), nethermind#14031 (debug_traceBlockByHash). Spencer and Jochem on 8253: behaviour only at the fork transition, so test it on as many devnets as possible.

Gaps to close before hegota-devnet-0

What we should build next

  • Cross-EIP tests: frames in inclusion lists (#3777), frames × 8250 × 8272.
  • FOCIL scenarios in assertoor and spamoor: censored txs, IL timing, equivocation.
  • Frame transaction spam and fuzzing on devnets.
  • RPC and tracing coverage for new tx types.
  • Fork-transition tests run on every devnet, not just the first.
  • Faster hive feedback per release: EngineX and WireX on the hegota groups.

These are proposals for discussion, not commitments. Pick owners on the next slide.
